Recorded simulator demonstrations, not CARLA or evidence of real-world safety. Runs end on completion, collision, off-route or 40 simulated seconds within 0.5 m. Jam NPC junction reservations are simple kinematic coordination, not traffic lights; crossing cars may yield to the ego. The collision guard coordinates NPCs only and does not shield the ego. The 10 new v14 jam runs use the already observed scenario seed and changed traffic geometry; treat them as development demonstrations, separate from earlier results.
